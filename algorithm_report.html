1 · Executive summary — is it your algorithm?

Yes — the core is exactly your idea, and it is the default path. The repo encodes a corpus of ~1000 prompts through the SD1.5 CLIP text encoder once, fits an independent Gaussian per coordinate of the (77, 768) conditioning tensor (μ, σ per latent — 59,136 univariate Gaussians), samples fresh conditioning as c = μ + T·σ⊙z, and feeds it straight to the UNet via diffusers' prompt_embeds, bypassing the text encoder entirely. Good latents are saved as per-image .npz sidecars and can be refit into new distributions ("evolved branches"). Temperature scaling and sigma-clipping (a GAN-style truncation trick) are both there.

But your friend built substantially more around that core. The four biggest additions, in decreasing order of statistical significance:

  1. A correlated (low-rank PCA) sampler. The fit also stores the top N−1 principal components of the corpus cloud, and a pca sampler draws deviations inside that subspace — deliberately breaking your "uncorrelated" assumption to get more coherent samples, with a blend sampler interpolating the two covariances exactly.
  2. A calibrated distance gauge + shell sampling. Every sample gets an RMS z-score distance from the corpus center; samples can be floored ("never below d=1.0") or pinned to a target shell — a more surgical version of temperature.
  3. Formalized iteration. Your "save good latents → recompute statistics" loop exists in three flavors: a cross-entropy-method (CEM) evolution loop with an aesthetic scorer, a favorites-refit script, and an autonomous "frontier hunter" that optimizes a Pareto front of novelty × learned personal taste.
  4. A SLERP "hybrid" sampler that fuses two real corpus embeddings instead of sampling the Gaussian at all.

One notable restriction relative to your description: there is no per-coordinate sigma modulation. Temperature is a single global scalar on the whole deviation; you cannot skew or reshape individual σ's (the closest substitutes are PCA band selection and the equalize flag, which operate per principal axis, not per coordinate). Also note it is a single diagonal Gaussian, not a multi-modal mixture — no k>1 mixture fitting exists anywhere in the code. Details in §9.

3 · Stage 1 — Mining: harvesting CLIP conditioning

scripts/mine_distribution.py · semantic_anarchy/pipeline.py (SDModel.encode_prompts, lines 255–276)

  • Corpus: prompts_1000.txt — 1000 deterministic "good prompts" (regenerable via gen_prompts.py). Blank lines and # comments stripped.
  • Encoder call: the stock diffusers pipe.encode_prompt(..., do_classifier_free_guidance=False) in batches of 16. This is the standard SD1.5 conditioning: tokenize to exactly 77 tokens (BOS + text + EOS + padding), CLIP ViT-L/14 text encoder, final hidden states after the final layer norm. No pooling, no layer skipping.
  • Precision: embeddings are cast to float32 on the way out; the fit later promotes to float64.
  • Result: E of shape (N=1000, 77, 768). This is the only time the text encoder runs; generation never touches it.
Matches your design: these are precisely "the activations that normally feed into the unet" — the cross-attention conditioning tensor, recorded once per prompt.

4 · Stage 2 — Fitting: the statistical model

semantic_anarchy/distribution.py (EmbeddingDistribution.fit, lines 114–222)

4.1 The diagonal Gaussian (your model)

Default (per_token=True): one independent Gaussian per (token, feature) coordinate:

μt,f = (1/N) Σi Ei,t,f     σt,f = √( (1/N) Σi (Ei,t,f − μt,f)² )     σ ← max(σ, 10⁻⁴) 77 × 768 = 59,136 Gaussians
  • σ uses NumPy's default ddof = 0 (biased, 1/N) estimator; the PCA spectrum below uses 1/(N−1). A ~0.05% inconsistency at N=1000 — cosmetic, but worth knowing.
  • The std_floor = 1e-4 guards collapsed coordinates. Important side effect of the per-token fit: structurally constant positions (the BOS token embedding is identical for every prompt; late padding positions are nearly so) get σ ≈ 0, so samples automatically reproduce CLIP's structural tokens almost exactly. This is a quiet but significant reason per-token diagonal sampling works at all.
  • An alternative --pooled mode collapses the token axis into a single shared (768,) Gaussian (mean/std over all N·77 rows) — a stronger prior yielding "calmer" samples. Not the default.

4.2 The low-rank PCA structure (beyond your model)

The fit additionally flattens each embedding to D = 59,136 dims, centers by μ, and takes an economy SVD:

Xc = X − 1μᵀ = U S Vᵀ,    keep Vk ∈ ℝk×D (rows = top right-singular vectors),   sj = Sj / √(N−1) k = N−1 = 999 by default
  • pca_std[j] = s_j is the per-component standard deviation of the corpus along axis j. With the default n_components=None, all 999 non-trivial components are kept, so the PCA basis captures 100% of corpus variance (total_var is stored with the same 1/(N−1) normalization for the bookkeeping fraction).
  • This adds what your uncorrelated model deliberately threw away: the correlation structure between coordinates — i.e. the actual manifold directions the corpus varies along.

4.3 The retained raw corpus

Up to max_corpus = 256 raw embeddings are kept inside the saved distribution (random subset, fixed seed 0) to power the hybrid SLERP sampler. Everything is persisted to a compressed .npz plus a small .meta.json.

5 · Stage 3 — Sampling: four samplers + post-ops

semantic_anarchy/distribution.py (sample, lines 225–366)

All samplers share one master formula (except hybrid, which returns full samples):

c = μ + T · dev T = --temperature, global scalar

5.1 diagonal — your original sampler (the default)

z ~ 𝒩(0, I) ∈ ℝ77×768,   z ← truncate(z),   dev = σ ⊙ z

Independent per-coordinate draws. At T = 1 this reproduces each marginal exactly but ignores all correlations — "maximal anarchy," off-manifold by construction.

5.2 pca — draw within the corpus subspace

a ~ 𝒩(0, Im),   a ← truncate(a),   dev = (a ⊙ scale) Vband,   Vband = V[lo : lo+m]
  • Axis band selection: --comp-lo / --components pick a window of principal axes. comp_lo = 0 rides the dominant (generic, "tasteful") directions; setting it higher (the drives use 40–200) skips those and rides idiosyncratic minor axes — the "weird-but-coherent" knob.
  • Natural scale (default): scale = s_band, each axis at its own corpus variance. --equalize: every selected axis instead gets the same magnitude, the RMS of the full spectrum: scale_j = √(mean(s²)) — so minor axes, normally inaudible, are expressed at full strength.
  • Since samples stay in span(V), T > 1 extrapolates along real corpus directions — outside the convex hull but on the manifold's axes. This is the repo's flagship "coherent chaos" mode.

5.3 blend — exact covariance interpolation

dev = √λ · devpca + √(1−λ) · devdiag   ⇒   Cov = λ·VTS²V + (1−λ)·diag(σ²) λ = --coherence ∈ [0,1]

Because the two deviations are drawn independently, the √-weighting makes the resulting covariance an exact linear interpolation of the two covariance matrices. λ=0 and λ=1 reproduce the pure samplers bit-for-bit (same RNG consumption).

5.4 hybrid — SLERP concept fusion (no Gaussian at all)

pick a, b from the 256 retained corpus rows,   t ~ U(0.3, 0.7)
c = slerp(a, b, t) + 0.15 · T · σ ⊙ z

The SLERP interpolates direction on the unit hypersphere of the flattened 59,136-dim vectors and lerps the magnitudes, so fusions keep corpus-typical norm instead of collapsing toward the center like a plain lerp. The additive jitter (15% of a temperature-T diagonal deviation) keeps children off exact corpus chords. This sampler stays inside the hull.

5.5 Truncation (the σ-clipping you described)

distribution.py _truncate, lines 26–41

--truncation τ applies to the standard-normal coefficients before scaling: any |z| > τ is resampled up to 8 times, then hard-clipped to ±τ — the GAN truncation trick. It applies identically to per-coordinate z's (diagonal) and per-component a's (pca), so the knob means the same thing everywhere. Off by default.

Separately, project() (lines 462–471) implements "creation through projection": whiten any arbitrary tensor by (μ, σ), clip to ±3σ, unwhiten — pulling out-of-domain tensors onto the typical set.

5.6 The distance gauge and shell operations

distribution.py distance/retarget lines 368–454 · backend.py floor_distance/retarget lines 151–171

d(x) = √( meant,f [ ((x−μ)/σ̃)² ] ),    σ̃ = max(σ, 0.1·mean(σ))

An RMS z-score whitened by the fitted Gaussian — a typical corpus sample sits near d ≈ 1, a temperature-T diagonal sample near d ≈ T. The σ floor at 10% of the mean σ prevents near-constant coordinates (BOS, padding) from exploding the gauge for anchors not minted from this exact fit. Two post-ops use it, both preserving direction and rescaling only the radius:

  • --min-distance f: any sample with d < f is pushed out onto the floor: x ← μ + (x−μ)·f/d. (The hunter runs with f = 1.0 — "never dip into the bland corpus core.")
  • --target-distance t: shell sampling — every sample is rescaled to d = t exactly, so you draw on the ring where your keepers live while the sampler still supplies the direction.

Every generated image's JSON sidecar records its d, which is what makes taste-band targeting possible later.

6 · Stage 4 — Decoding through the UNet

semantic_anarchy/pipeline.py (SDModel.generate_from_embeddings, lines 289–367) · scripts/generate.py

  • Each sampled c (77×768) is passed as prompt_embeds to the stock StableDiffusionPipeline — no forked diffusers, no hooks. Images are generated one at a time, seed = batch_seed + i per image for independent reproducibility. Batch seed is random (and printed) unless --seed is given.
  • CFG: defaults steps=30, guidance=7.5, 512×512. The negative branch is always the cached empty-prompt encoding for SD1.5. Statistically this matters: with guidance g, the effective denoising direction is ε(∅) + g·(ε(c) − ε(∅)), so what gets amplified 7.5× is the sampled conditioning relative to the empty prompt — CFG acts as a second, image-space "temperature" on top of T.
  • Scheduler is the pipeline default (PNDM for SD1.5) unless --scheduler ddim|euler|euler_a|dpm; the drives run DDIM. Safety checker is disabled.
  • Sidecars: every image gets {stem}.npz holding its exact conditioning tensor (this is your "save the good latents" — saved for all images, curation happens later) and {stem}.json with every knob plus the distance d.
  • Optional entropy injection: --init-dir starts each image img2img from a random "good init" at strength 0.7, or --init-mode embedding injects the init through IP-Adapter (CLIP image embedding via cross-attention) while still denoising from pure noise. Plus an img2img hires-refine (refine_image) that reuses the sidecar conditioning so upscales reinforce the same content.
Quirk: SD15Backend.generate accepts a neg_mode argument (mean|empty|zeros) but ignores it — the corpus-mean negative trick is only wired up for SDXL (backend.py line 243–252 vs 311–324). On SD1.5 the negative is always the empty prompt, whatever you pass.

8 · Selection & evolution: three feedback loops

8.1 The refit primitive (your "recompute statistics on keepers")

distribution.py refit_from_elites, lines 474–498

μ′ = mean(elites)     σ′ = β·σbase + (1−β)·std(elites),   β = 0.25     σ′ ← max(σ′, 0.1·σbase)

Crucially this is not a raw refit: the elite spread is blended with 25% of the base corpus spread and floored at 10% of it, so branches specialize without collapsing to a point. The refit result carries no PCA; evolve_favorites.py explicitly grafts the base corpus PCA axes and retained corpus back onto the branch so all samplers keep working on it.

8.2 Loop A — CEM evolution with a scorer scripts/evolve.py

semantic_anarchy/evolve.py · aesthetic.py

for each generation (default 10):
    population = dist.sample(64, T, truncation)     # diagonal sampler
    scores     = scorer(population)                 # decode → score images
    elites     = top 25% by score
    dist       = dist.refit_from_elites(elites)     # β = 0.25

Scorers: aesthetic = the LAION improved-aesthetic-predictor (CLIP ViT-L/14 image features, L2-normalized, through the 1024→128→64→16→1 linear head — the same head Eden used), human = interactive 1–10 rating, random = plumbing test.

8.3 Loop B — favorites branch scripts/evolve_favorites.py

Collects the .npz latents of every ★-starred image (≥3 required), runs one refit_from_elites on them, grafts the corpus PCA back, saves as outputs/dist_evolved, and samples it (default: diagonal sampler, i.e. tightest to the elite spread). This is the closest analog to your manual iterate-on-keepers workflow.

8.4 Loop C — the autonomous frontier hunter webui/hunt_drive.py + scripts/resonance.py

An unattended generative-recommender loop on top of the web dashboard:

  • Novelty: every gallery image gets a CLIP ViT-L embedding; novelty = 1 − cosine similarity to its nearest neighbor in the gallery (catches motif repetition, which the corpus-distance gauge cannot).
  • Resonance: a balanced L2-regularized logistic regression (400 full-batch steps, lr 0.5, λ=10⁻³) on CLIP features, positives = your stars (≥8 required), negatives = 5× randomly sampled non-starred. Output: P(you'd star it) for every image.
  • Frontier: successive Pareto layers of novelty × resonance ("nothing else is both newer AND more you"), up to 120 images.
  • Strategy portfolio per round (deliberate variance, never one shell): 30% free-range pca at T ∈ [1.4, 2.2]; 25% taste-band shell targeting (fit a Gaussian to your starred images' recorded distances, draw a target d from it, clip to [1.3, 2.4] — needs ≥10 stars); 25% weird minor axes (comp_lo ∈ {40…200}, equalize, T ∈ [1.05, 1.4]); 20% hybrid fusion. All with min_distance = 1.0. Frontier images that haven't been visited get neighborhood children (r = 0.25).

A sibling guided_drive.py does exploitation-weighted knob sampling from the star sidecars (add-one smoothing, 15% exploration floor) — though its priors currently steer hard toward SDXL.

10 · Statistical fine print & potential gotchas

  1. Estimator noise: 59,136 per-coordinate σ's from N=1000 samples means each σ carries ~4.5% relative sampling error (√(1/2N)); harmless for generation, but per-token statistics are far noisier than pooled ones would be. The PCA spectrum's tail (components ~500–999) is essentially noise-level and mostly matters when equalize amplifies it.
  2. ddof mismatch: diagonal σ uses 1/N, PCA σ and total_var use 1/(N−1). Negligible at N=1000; would bite for tiny elite refits — except refit_from_elites doesn't compute PCA at all.
  3. Distance gauge σ-floor: d(x) uses σ̃ = max(σ, 0.1·mean(σ)). Consequently structural coordinates (BOS/padding, true σ≈10⁻⁴) contribute almost nothing to d — the gauge effectively measures distance in the "live" subspace. Good for robustness; means d is not exactly the Mahalanobis distance of the fitted model.
  4. Diagonal + truncation interaction: truncation acts on z before temperature, so --temperature 2 --truncation 2 still reaches 4σ in raw units. The knobs compose multiplicatively, not min().
  5. Blend covariance claim is exact only because the pca and diagonal deviations are drawn independently — the code does this correctly (two separate RNG draws, √λ weights).
  6. Hybrid jitter scale: the 0.15·T·σ noise in the hybrid sampler means temperature has a much weaker (7×-attenuated) effect there than in the other samplers; hybrid images cluster near d ≈ 1 regardless of T.
  7. neg_mode is a no-op on SD1.5 (always empty-prompt negative), despite the CLI accepting it. Only SDXL implements corpus-mean negatives.
  8. Elite refits keep the base PCA verbatim: an evolved branch samples PCA deviations around your mean but along the base corpus's axes and scales — the covariance never adapts to your taste, only the center and diagonal spread do.
  9. Per-token vs pooled is a real fork: the shipped distribution is per-token. If your old implementation pooled over token positions (768 Gaussians instead of 59,136), the sample texture will differ noticeably — per-token preserves positional structure (BOS/EOS/padding), pooled scrambles it.
  10. Corpus retention cap: only 256 of 1000 raw embeddings survive into the saved distribution; the hybrid sampler fuses from that subset only.

13 · New ideas — the next round of experiments

Everything below follows from one observation: the machine has three levers, and almost all the work so far has pushed only the first one.

  1. Lever 1 — where the deviation comes from: which distribution, which basis, which marginals. This is where diagonal / pca / blend / hybrid / refit all live. Heavily explored.
  2. Lever 2 — how the deviation is structured across the tensor: the token axis, sparsity, asymmetry, on- vs off-manifold energy. Almost untouched — and the old latent_magic.py tricks that were lost (percentile clamp, token pinning, mix_latents) all lived here.
  3. Lever 3 — how the UNet is driven: the negative branch, the guidance scale, the time axis of denoising, feedback through the image. Nearly untouched — and everything here gets multiplied by g = 7.5 in ε-space before it reaches pixels, so it's the highest-gain lever in the system.

One meta-principle before the list: gradient descent needs a gradient. Every idea below is sized to be testable in 1–2 batches of 16–32 images against a fixed seed panel, and the measurement ideas (#19–#21) exist to make those batches legible data points instead of vibes. Tags on each card: flag = reuses existing flags or ≤10 lines · script = standalone script, core untouched · sampler = new sampler or fit change · pipeline = touches the decode loop · ★–★★★ = expected leverage.

A · Drive the UNet differently lever 3 — the 7.5× multiplier

#1 Fabricated negatives: point the CFG vector where you want it

flag★★★

The negative branch is a free second embedding slot that currently always holds the empty prompt (neg_mode is a literal no-op on SD1.5 — fixing that is the first commit). Since the guided direction is ε(c) − ε(neg), choosing neg chooses what gets amplified 7.5×. Three settings, in increasing weirdness: (a) neg = μ, the corpus mean — guidance now amplifies "how this sample differs from the average prompt", actively suppressing generic content (already implemented for SDXL; port it); (b) aligned negative, neg = μ − γ·(c − μ) — the guidance difference becomes ≈ (1+γ)× the sampled deviation itself, so you amplify your deviation in ε-space without raising T in embedding space, where high T degrades structure; (c) neg = an independent hot sample — every image becomes a tug-of-war between two anarchic points, the old numpy code's fabricated-uc trick reborn.

Combines with: every sampler; #2 (sweep g per neg-mode); #12 (neg = −taste direction).

#2 Guidance scale as a first-class anarchy knob

flag★★

Zero new code — --guidance already exists and nobody has swept it. §6 already establishes that CFG is a second, image-space temperature; treat it as one. Grid (T, g) with T ∈ {0.8, 1.2, 1.8} × g ∈ {1, 3, 7.5, 15, 30}, 16 fixed seeds per cell, one contour plot of keeper-rate. Include the exotic corners: g ∈ (0, 1) (under-guidance: the sampled conditioning as a whisper) and g < 0 (anti-guidance: denoise away from the sample — genuinely unexplored territory that costs one afternoon).

Combines with: everything, forever — this calibrates the baseline all other ideas are judged against.

#3 Conditioning schedules: vary c over the denoising trajectory

pipeline★★★

All 30 steps currently see the same c — but diffusion decides composition in the first ~30% of steps and texture in the rest. Swapping embeddings mid-run (diffusers' callback_on_step_end can mutate prompt_embeds; no fork needed) splits anarchy into two independently controllable phases: hot→mild = alien layout rendered competently (likely the grail for "weird but stunning"); mild→hot = a normal scene with alien surfaces; continuous slerp c(t) = an entire latent walk inside one image. This one mechanism turns every pair of existing samplers into a 2-D grid of (early, late) choices — the single biggest combinatorial expansion available.

Combines with: any two samplers as endpoints; #1 (schedule the negative too); #11 (mantra early, gaussian late).

#4 The dream loop: iterated img2img with re-sampled conditioning

script★★

Decode a batch; feed each output back as its own init image (--init-dir machinery, strength ≈ 0.55) with a fresh sampled c each round; run 5–8 rounds and keep the whole trajectory. The image accumulates structure that no single conditioning asked for — attractor-seeking drift through image space × conditioning space, deep-dream style. Cheap variant: keep c fixed and only re-noise (does the UNet reinforce or erode the anarchy?); expensive variant: re-sample c from the neighborhood of the previous round's c so image and conditioning drift together.

Combines with: #12 (steer each round up the taste gradient); #19 (score trajectories, not frames).

B · Fix the marginals, shape the deviation levers 1+2

#5 Bootstrap marginals: sample the empirical distribution directly

sampler★★

CLIP activations are skewed and heavy-tailed; a Gaussian is the wrong marginal for every one of the 59,136 coordinates. Replace σ⊙z with direct resampling: for each coordinate independently, draw one of its 1000 recorded corpus values. Independence (the anarchy) is preserved; the marginals become exact — skew, kurtosis, bimodality, and the old latent_magic.py percentile envelope are all inherited for free, in ~30 lines with no re-mining (the full E matrix is already on disk). Temperature generalizes cleanly as rank stretch: map each draw's corpus rank r through r′ = ½ + T·(r − ½), clipping or linearly extrapolating the tails — T > 1 now means "prefer the tails of the real distribution" rather than "assume Gaussian tails exist".

Combines with: #7 (bootstrap the off-manifold part only); #9 (crossover then bootstrap-jitter).

#6 Two-piece skew fit: the per-coordinate σ modulation, finally

sampler★

The original design called for "skewing the distribution by modulating the sigmas" and §9 shows it was never built. Minimal faithful version: fit σ⁺ and σ⁻ per coordinate (std of positive and negative residuals separately), then sample a sign and a half-normal. One extra array in the fit, a --skew flag, and asymmetry is restored — plus a free new knob: exaggerate the skew (σ⁺/σ⁻ ratio raised to a power λ) to push every coordinate harder toward its naturally heavy side.

Combines with: any diagonal-based sampler; largely subsumed by #5 — run both once, keep the winner.

#7 Two-temperature on/off-manifold split — the diagnostic experiment

sampler★★★

Decompose the diagonal deviation into its projection onto the PCA subspace and the orthogonal remainder: dev = PVdev + (I − PV)dev, and give each part its own temperature (Ton, Toff). This generalizes diagonal/pca/blend into one 2-knob family — and the corner (0, T) is a sampler that exists nowhere yet: pure off-manifold deviations, orthogonal to every direction the corpus actually varies along. One batch at each of the four corners answers the deepest open question in the project: does the good weirdness live on the manifold, off it, or on the diagonal between? Every future sampling decision inherits the answer. Cost: one matvec per sample against the already-stored V.

Combines with: #5, #8; the (Ton, Toff) map belongs in #21's response surface.

#8 Sparse axis cocktails: few strong concepts beat diffuse noise

sampler★★

A Gaussian draw over 999 components spreads energy everywhere — which may be exactly why high-T images read as "textured soup". Real prompts are sparse in concept space: a few strong words. Mirror that: pick K ∈ {2…8} components at random (optionally within a comp_lo band), give each a large amplitude ±(3–6)·sj, zero everywhere else. Predicted signature: images with a few strong alien concepts instead of uniform strangeness. Then the killer follow-up, nearly free because sidecars record everything: log which axes appear in starred images → a per-axis leaderboard → bias the cocktail toward proven axes. That's a bandit over basis vectors — taste-directed exploration with no model fitting at all.

Combines with: #7 (sparse on-manifold + dense off-manifold); #3 (different cocktail early vs late).

C · Token-axis operators lever 2 — the lost dimension

#9 Resurrect mix_latents: per-token row crossover

sampler★★

The one demonstrably lost operator (§12.2): pick n ∈ {4…128} parents from the 256 retained corpus rows; for each of the 77 token positions independently, copy that entire 768-dim row from a random parent; finish with mild temperature + truncation. Row-level copying keeps each token internally coherent (unlike per-coordinate independence) while scrambling the "sentence" — a genetic crossover in prompt space that no current sampler approximates. It worked four years ago; it's 15 lines to bring back; n itself is a coherence knob (n=2 ≈ hybrid, n=128 ≈ token-level anarchy).

Combines with: #5 (bootstrap jitter on top); #13 (parents = starred latents only — taste crossover).

#10 Token-position temperature profiles

flag★★

Promote the global scalar T to a 77-vector Tt (one broadcast multiply). Three profiles worth a batch each: (a) structural pinning — T=0 at BOS/EOS/padding, hot in the content span (the old code's token-pinning trick, now soft and tunable); (b) random smooth profiles — sample 8 spline knots, upsample to 77, so each image gets its own "where in the sentence the madness lives"; (c) one-hot-token — everything at T=0.3 except 3–5 random content positions at T=4: a plausible sentence with a few words replaced by static. This is the most legible form of the per-coordinate σ modulation the original design wanted — and profiles are nameable, starrable, evolvable objects.

Combines with: every sampler; #6/#5 (profile × marginal); the hunter's strategy portfolio.

#11 Mantra conditioning: one concept, chanted

flag★

Sample a single plausible content row (768-dim — from the pooled fit, or steal one token from a real embedding), tile it across all ~50 content positions, keep BOS/EOS/padding at μ. Cross-attention sees one concept repeated 50× — a conditioning state no text can produce, since real sentences never repeat a token embedding exactly (position is baked into CLIP's output values). Variants: alternate 2–3 rows (a chord instead of a note); tile with slowly increasing noise per position (a concept dissolving as the sentence proceeds). Ten lines, deterministic weirdness, completely orthogonal to every existing sampler.

Combines with: #3 (mantra early → gaussian late: alien composition, familiar texture).

D · Taste geometry use the stars as directions, not just centers

#12 The contrastive steering vector

script★★★

refit_from_elites moves the center toward your stars; this extracts the direction. Fit LDA / logistic regression in conditioning space — starred latents vs random non-starred, done in the top-50 PCA coordinates so 14 stars are enough — and take the unit vector w. Then every sampler, present and future, gets one added term: c ← c + α·w. Unlike a refit branch it needs no bookkeeping, composes with everything, and is sign-flippable: −α·w is anti-taste, which is exactly what the novelty objective wants when the gallery starts repeating itself. Recompute w in seconds whenever the star set grows. This is the cheapest possible way to make taste a continuous knob instead of a discrete branch.

Combines with: everything via one addition; #1c (neg = μ − α·w: guidance itself pushes up the taste gradient).

#13 Star-manifold sampling: learn the covariance of your taste

script★★

§10.8's gotcha — evolved branches keep the base corpus PCA, so the covariance never adapts — fixed properly: project the starred latents into the top-m PCA coordinates (m ≈ 30, small enough that ~14 stars still constrain it), fit a small full-covariance Gaussian there with shrinkage toward its diagonal, sample coefficients from that, map back through V. Where the refit learns "where your taste is centered", this learns "which directions your taste varies along" — correlations of preference, not just location. Falls back to the current behavior as shrinkage → 1, so it strictly generalizes the existing branch mechanics.

Combines with: #8 (sparse cocktails from the taste covariance's own eigenvectors); #14 (as its initialization).

#14 CMA-ES in coefficient space: an optimizer that can actually optimize

script★★

The existing CEM loop mutates all 59,136 coordinates blindly — evolution strategies don't work in that dimension. But in PCA coefficient space the problem is 40-dimensional, which is exactly where CMA-ES shines: optimize a ∈ ℝ⁴⁰ (leading components, or a chosen weird band), score = w₁·taste-model + w₂·novelty (both scorers already exist), population 24, ~15 generations ≈ 360 decodes — one overnight run on the existing hardware. Unlike the hunter (which samples strategies), this actually climbs. Seed the initial covariance from #13 and it starts from your taste instead of from zero.

Combines with: #19 (its fitness function is the report card); any sampler as the initialization.

E · New distributions to mine change what "typical" means

#15 Mixture fit: make the "mixture of Gaussians" literal at last

sampler★★

§1 notes the original description said mixture but only a single Gaussian was ever fit. Do it: k-means (k ≈ 8–20) on the PCA coefficients of the 1000 corpus embeddings → per-cluster μk, σk (and optionally local PCA). Three new moves fall out: sample within a far-from-center cluster (themed anarchy — all draws share a flavor); sample the chord between two cluster centers ± noise (hybridizing prompt regions instead of prompt pairs); and per-cluster temperature (which neighborhoods of prompt space tolerate heat before collapsing?). Fit-side script only; the samplers reuse the existing blend machinery.

Combines with: #9 (crossover parents from two different clusters); #18 (different cluster per encoder stream).

#16 Adversarial corpora & distribution arithmetic

script★★

Mining takes minutes, so mine contrast corpora: dist_boring (500 dull literal captions) and dist_weird (surrealist poetry, glossolalia, non-Latin scripts, unicode soup, dense technical jargon). Then operate on fits, not samples: repulsion — project the top boring-vs-base LDA directions out of every deviation, or add −β·(μboring − μ) so guidance-space never drifts toward stock imagery; fit interpolation — μ ← μ + β(μweird − μ) with geometric σ interpolation, an "anarchy prior" slider. Distribution-space arithmetic is a whole new plane of knobs, and each new corpus is one mine_distribution.py run.

Combines with: #12 (boring-repulsion is a corpus-level steering vector); #1a (neg = μboring — guidance flees boredom directly).

F · Cross-model SDXL · Flux

#17 Paired-corpus translation: teleport gems between models

script★★

Encode the same 1000 prompts through SD1.5, SDXL, and Flux's T5; the corpora are then row-aligned, so a ridge regression (or a map between the two PCA coefficient spaces — just 999×999) translates any conditioning tensor from one model's space to another's. Two payoffs: every discovered SD1.5 gem gets a free high-res SDXL/Flux render attempt from its own latent, not a re-roll; and the transfer success rate is itself the finding — if anarchic directions survive translation, weirdness is a property of language geometry; if not, it's a property of each model's decoder. Either answer shapes everything downstream.

Combines with: #12 (translate the taste vector once, reuse everywhere); the existing favorites workflow (translate all stars in bulk).

#18 Dual-stream dissonance: conditioning states no text can reach

pipeline★★★

SDXL conditions on two text encoders (CLIP-L + OpenCLIP bigG) plus a pooled vector; Flux on a T5 sequence plus a CLIP pooled vector. Every text prompt ever written produces consistent streams — so the inconsistent region of conditioning space is virgin territory by construction, unreachable even in principle by prompting. Sample the streams independently (different draws, different clusters from #15, or one stream real + one sampled); mismatch the pooled vector against the sequence; on Flux, fabricate only the T5 stream while keeping CLIP-pooled real (and vice versa) to isolate which stream carries the anarchy. The multi-tensor backend abstraction already exists — this is mostly plumbing flags through it. Arguably the purest expression of the project's premise.

Combines with: #3 (swap only one stream mid-denoise); #15; #17.

G · Make the gradient real measurement — the meta-idea

#19 Fixed seed panel + batch report card

script★★★

Freeze a panel of 16 seeds and render every experiment against it, so comparisons are paired and visual — same noise, different idea. Then a report card per batch, computed from data that already exists (sidecars + the hunter's CLIP features): taste-model mean, novelty mean, distance histogram, colorfulness, edge entropy, % near-duplicates within the batch. Emit one HTML strip per batch, ranked. This is the meta-idea that makes "gradient descent in idea space" literal: without it every batch is a vibe, with it every 20-minute batch is a data point, and the 21 ideas on this page become a measurable tournament instead of a pile of hunches. Build this first.

Combines with: all of the above — it's the referee.

#20 Legibility-cliff mapping: find the edge, per direction

script★★

The interesting images live just before coherence collapses — but §5.6's shell targeting assumes one radius fits all directions, and it almost certainly doesn't. Protocol, using only existing flags: fix a direction u (a PCA axis, a sparse cocktail, the steering vector, or one sampled deviation), render at --target-distance d ∈ {1.0, 1.4, …, 3.0}, and auto-score legibility (max CLIP similarity to the corpus images, or the taste model's confidence). The steepest drop is that direction's cliff — a single number per direction. Findings compound: keepers should cluster just before their cliff, so per-direction cliff estimates turn shell targeting from a global knob into an adaptive one, and "cliff distance" itself becomes a novelty-adjacent axis to explore along.

Combines with: #8 (cliff per cocktail axis → leaderboard column); the hunter's taste-band strategy (band per direction, not global).

#21 Knob-response surface: fit the experiment history itself

script★★

Every image already carries its complete knob vector (sampler, T, comp_lo, λ, g, d, truncation…) plus a starred bit — thousands of labeled points of "what settings produce keepers", sitting unused in the sidecar JSONs. Periodically fit a simple model over that ledger (logistic regression on knobs to start; a GP if it earns it), then print two things: the current argmax cell and the steepest uphill direction from the best-explored region. hunt_drive samples from a fixed strategy portfolio but never fits the history — this closes that loop, and the uphill direction is, literally, the gradient in knob space. An evening of pandas + sklearn.

Combines with: #19 (report card fields become features); feeds updated weights back into the hunter's portfolio.
